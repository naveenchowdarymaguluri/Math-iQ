# Privacy Policy for Math IQ

**Last updated: May 29, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Math IQ** mobile application ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use it. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

The App is published by the developer of Math IQ (package name `com.naveen.iqmath`).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

Math IQ is designed to work **without requiring you to create an account or provide personal details**. Your game progress (XP, levels, stars, streaks, achievements, and settings) is stored **locally on your device** and is not uploaded to us.

However, we and our third-party partners may automatically collect certain information to operate, improve, and monetize the App:

### a) Information collected automatically
- **Device and usage data** — device model, operating system version, language, app version, and crash/diagnostic logs.
- **Analytics events** — anonymous interactions such as levels played, sessions completed, and features used.
- **Advertising identifiers** — a resettable device advertising ID used to serve and measure ads.

### b) Information stored only on your device
- Game progress, scores, achievements, and preferences (via local storage). This data is removed when you uninstall the App or use the in-app "Reset Progress" option.

We do **not** intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos, or contacts.

Math IQ is a family-friendly educational math app and may appeal to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where the App is directed to children, we configure our advertising partners to serve only age-appropriate, non-personalized ads in line with the Google Play Families Policy and applicable laws (such as COPPA and GDPR-K).

If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
